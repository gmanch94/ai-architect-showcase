AI Vendor / Build-vs-Buy Assessment Matrix

Score up to 5 vendors plus your build option across 8 weighted criteria. Live ranking + comparative chart + build-vs-buy verdict.

Doctrine. Most AI vendor decisions are made on demo polish and reference logos. Both are noise. The signal is in 8 dimensions that determine whether you can operate the system 24 months from now: capability fit, data control, deployment options, 3-year TCO, lock-in, security, compliance, and roadmap. Score them; weight them; defend the number.

1. Pick a preset or start blank

Presets fill realistic 2025-2026 candidates. Edit names, scores, and weights freely.

2. Score vendors (1 = weak, 5 = strong)

Total ≥ 4.0 — strong fit 3.0 - 4.0 — viable, watch gaps < 3.0 — disqualify or remediate
Vendor Capability fit1: misses core use case · 3: covers main path, gaps on edges · 5: solves the actual use case with margin Data control1: data trains shared model, no opt-out · 3: opt-out available, data still leaves tenant · 5: data stays in your tenant, contractual no-train Deployment1: multi-tenant SaaS only · 3: VPC / dedicated tenant option · 5: SaaS + VPC + on-prem / hybrid available 3-yr TCO1: licenses + integration + ops far above budget · 3: at budget, integration costs uncertain · 5: predictable, well under budget over 3 years Lock-in1: proprietary APIs, no data export · 3: export available, migration painful · 5: open standards, portable data, low switching cost Security1: no certifications · 3: SOC2 Type II · 5: SOC2 + ISO 27001 + sector certs (HIPAA / FedRAMP) as needed Compliance1: no AI-Act / GDPR posture · 3: GDPR + AI-Act conformity in progress · 5: full EU AI Act conformity, sector-specific (HIPAA / PCI) ready Roadmap1: opaque roadmap, vendor funding risk · 3: published roadmap, partial alignment · 5: roadmap matches your needs, stable vendor Total

How to read this

PatternWhat it means
Buy wins by > 0.5The vendor edge is real. Build only if you have a strategic reason (proprietary data moat, regulatory mandate, core differentiation). Otherwise buy and redirect engineering to differentiation.
Buy wins by < 0.3Inside the noise. Decide on non-numeric factors: vendor relationship, exit strategy, team appetite. Run a paid POC on top two before signing.
Build winsEither no vendor fits your data/compliance posture, or capability fit is so high on your build that vendor delta isn't worth the lock-in. Validate that your build TCO score is honest — most internal builds underestimate by 2-3x.
All options < 3.0Market isn't ready. Defer decision; revisit in 6 months. Don't force a bad choice because a slide deadline says you must "have an AI strategy."
