EU AI Act Risk-Tier Classifier

Answer three yes/no questions about your primary AI use case. Result is indicative, not legal advice — consult counsel for binding classification.

Q1. Does the system perform social scoring, manipulative subliminal techniques, untargeted facial-image scraping, real-time biometric ID in public spaces, or emotion recognition in workplaces / schools?
Q2. Does it make or materially inform decisions about employment, credit, insurance, education access, essential public services, law enforcement, migration, justice, or safety components of regulated products (medical devices, vehicles, critical infrastructure)?
Q3. Does it interact directly with people (chatbot), generate or manipulate synthetic content (text, image, audio, video), or use emotion-recognition / biometric-categorization outside the unacceptable-risk scope above?
