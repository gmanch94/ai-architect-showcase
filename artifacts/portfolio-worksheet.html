Score AI use-cases across 3 horizons (H1: cost takeout · H2: revenue lift · H3: new business model). Live ranking + bubble chart.

Doctrine. Treat AI as a portfolio, not a project. Run H1 (cost takeout, <12 mo), H2 (revenue lift, 12–24 mo), H3 (new business model, 24–36 mo) in parallel. Most enterprise AI value lives in H1 — don't skip it chasing H3.

How to read this

QuadrantAction
High BV + High DRShip now. These belong in H1. Don't over-engineer — pick the simplest method that works (logistic regression often beats deep learning here).
High BV + Low DRInvest in data plumbing first. The "data > models" doctrine (Halevy, Norvig, Pereira 2009) says model choice matters less than data quality and quantity. Treat this as an H2 data project disguised as an AI project.
Low BV + High DRDe-prioritize. Just because data is ready doesn't mean it's worth building. Free up resources for H1.
Low BV + Low DRKill or park. Either the case isn't real or you're 2 years from being able to do it.
