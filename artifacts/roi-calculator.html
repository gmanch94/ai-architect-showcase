How to read this

VerdictMeaning
NPV > 0, payback < 18 moDefensible business case. Ship it — but pressure-test the lift % with a 60-day pilot before signing the multi-year license.
NPV > 0, payback 18-30 moMarginal. The case lives or dies on the lift assumption. Run a measured pilot first. Don't fund based on vendor-quoted productivity numbers.
NPV < 0 or payback > 30 moThe math doesn't work at this lift. Either the use case is wrong, the headcount is too small, or ongoing costs are too high. Re-scope before reapplying.
Sensitivity bars cluster near zeroProject value is fragile to assumptions. A 5-point swing in lift flips the verdict. Treat as an experimental H1 spend, not a strategic bet.

Companion artifacts: portfolio worksheet · readiness scorecard · executive briefing

Assumptions. Linear productivity ramp from 0 to full lift over the ramp window. Benefits and ongoing costs occur annually (mid-year convention not applied). Pre-tax; no terminal value beyond Year 3. One-time cost incurred at t=0 (undiscounted). NPV = sum of discounted net cash flows. Payback uses undiscounted cumulative net cash flow against the one-time cost. Headcount and salary held constant over the horizon. Currency: USD.
