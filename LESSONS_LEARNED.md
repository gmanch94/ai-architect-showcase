**[2026-07-11] Forward-dated regulatory claims rot fastest — re-verify, don't just re-tense.** Staleness refresh found the HR checklist asserting "Colorado AI Act SB24-205, effective 30 Jun 2026." By Jul 2026 that was doubly wrong: a federal court froze enforcement (Apr 2026) and the legislature replaced it with SB 26-189 (eff 1 Jan 2027). Lesson: any law whose effective date has passed since the last touch needs a fresh source check — it may have been delayed, frozen, or superseded, not merely "now in effect." Model lineups rot the same way (mindmap still said "GPT-4o" while xAI/Grok was missing entirely). On each refresh, grep forward dates + model names and re-verify against the web before editing; use family-level model names ("GPT series", not point SKUs) to slow the rot.
